## Findings that shape the plan (verified by reading files)

**F1 — The quest contract lives INSIDE ComfyNetworkSense, not beside it.**
ComfyQuestLab.csproj links 6 files from ../ComfyNetworkSense/Core/:
- Core/Models/TrackedQuest.cs (46 ln)
- Core/Models/QuestEvent.cs (96 ln)
- Core/Services/QuestViewLoader.cs (391 ln)
- Core/Services/QuestTriggerEvaluator.cs (219 ln)
- Core/Services/QuestEventCatalog.g.cs (158 ln, generated by tools/component-packets)
- Core/Services/QuestAuthoring.cs (357 ln)
All are namespace ComfyNetworkSense, Unity-free, and are ALSO compiled into the shipping
ComfyNetworkSense.dll (SDK default glob). So quest source flows INTO the networksense
assembly. **Topology gap flagged:** the two decided packages do not cover these 6 files.
Resolution (stays within the decided NuGet mechanism): move the 6 files to comfy-quest and
ship them as **source-only contentFiles inside the Comfy.Quest.Contracts package**
(contentFiles/cs/any/, buildAction=Compile). ComfyNetworkSense PackageReferences it and
keeps compiling the identical source into its single BepInEx DLL — the "compile the SAME
contract" philosophy and single-DLL distribution are both preserved. Assembly consumers
(Companion) that only want the compiled ComfyQuestContracts.dll set
ExcludeAssets="contentFiles" on the reference.

**F2 — Transport-contracts package must be a source package too.**
ComfyNetworkSense.csproj:71 compiles Lumberjacks/src/Game.Contracts/Valheim/ValheimRoutedRpcAdmissions.cs
into the mod. Verified netstandard2.0-clean: BCL-only usings, C#12 syntax gated by LangVersion 12,
no Unity/ASP.NET types. The 4 policy files (ZdoIntegrationContract.cs, ZdoBandPolicy.cs,
ZdoFanoutPolicy.cs, VehicleSnapshotRelevanceSet.cs) are likewise Unity-free (System +
Collections.Generic only). They are compiled into the mod runtime AND linked by
tools/authority-lab/src/AuthorityLab csproj lines 11-14. So Comfy.Transport.Contracts
(home lumberjacks-platform) ships the 5 files as source contentFiles: the mod compiles them in
via package; authority-lab keeps same-repo relative links after the 4 files' canonical home moves
to Lumberjacks/src/Game.Contracts/Policies/. Determinism note: package sources resolve under the
NuGet cache; add PathMap $(NuGetPackageRoot)=C:\nuget\ to ComfyNetworkSense.csproj and
ComfyQuestLab.csproj so the SHA-verified single-DLL lanes stay byte-deterministic. Namespace of
the 4 policy files stays ComfyNetworkSense (renaming is the later refactor, out of scope).
