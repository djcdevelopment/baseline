> (batch rhythm, days rather than minutes).

This is an alpha environment for a community of creators, builders, and players.
The goal of the tools tested here is simple: make it easier to keep caring, and
give you back the hours you'd otherwise spend on maintenance so you get to be a
player and host again.

Because we're building this together, it helps to be clear about how support
works. A dead server at 3am shouldn't cost your trust — as long as you know what
to expect.

## What "alpha" means here

This project is run by exactly one operator (Derek). It is best-effort, and it is
built out in the open. Whether you connect via the self-service onboarding page
(comfy-p7.duckdns.org) or test a new guild recipe, you'll see wobbles — sometimes
things break. Integrations, fixes, and demos often happen live on stream. When a
failure happens live, it isn't an apology or a crisis; it's just part of the
alpha. Seeing the gears turn and watching the system get built in the open is the
deal. Occasional turbulence is how we find the right path.

## The response rhythm

When you report a bug or request a feature, it goes into a queue. We do a **weekly
sweep** of feedback rather than instant, on-demand hotfixes. You can expect to
hear back, or see movement, by the next weekly sweep — sometimes sooner on
stream. This steady rhythm is what keeps the project sustainable and the operator
un-burned-out. Full detail on how feedback is processed: [the weekly
rhythm](weekly-rhythm.md).

## How to report an issue

Send it to the operator in the community Discord (the bug/feedback channel). To
track it down quickly without exhausting back-and-forth, please include these
three things — you can copy-paste this list:

1. **The server time** — roughly when it happened.
2. **The quest name** — if it's quest-related.
3. **Your BepInEx log** — found in your Valheim install at `BepInEx/LogOutput.log`.

## What NOT to expect

To protect the people involved and honor the art this community makes, we protect
everyone's time — which means being honest about boundaries. During alpha, please
**don't expect**:

- **24/7 or instant fixes.** There are no SLAs or guaranteed uptime numbers. If a
  server goes down late at night, it may stay down until the next day.
- **Perfection.** The tools will break. That's not a failure of the community or
  the design; it's the physics of building something new.
- **Surveillance or mandates.** Nothing here audits you or forces a new way to
  play. These tools are opt-in — you use them only if they remove your toil. You
  own your outputs.

We're building this to give you relief, permission, and time. Thank you for
testing it with us.
