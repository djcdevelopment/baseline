# What to expect — how we build this together

This is an alpha environment for a community of creators, builders, and players.
The goal of the tools tested here is simple: make it easier to keep caring, and
give you back the hours you'd otherwise spend on maintenance so you get to be a
player and host again.

Because we're building this together, it helps to be clear about how support
works. A dead server at 3am shouldn't cost your trust — as long as you know what
to expect.

## What "alpha" means here

This project is run by exactly one operator (Derek). It is best-effort, and it is
built out in the open. Whether you're running a tool from the
[Workbench](https://am4.tail8e749c.ts.net/workbench) or testing a new guild recipe,
you'll see wobbles — sometimes things break. Integrations, fixes, and demos often
happen live on stream. When a failure happens live, it isn't an apology or a
crisis; it's just part of the alpha. Seeing the gears turn and watching the system get built in the open is the
deal. Occasional turbulence is how we find the right path.

## The response rhythm

When you report a bug or request a feature, it goes into a queue. Feedback is worked in
**batches — days rather than minutes** — not as instant, on-demand hotfixes. Every
thread gets read; replies come when the operator checks in. If a thread goes quiet for a
few days, that's pacing, not someone going silent on you. This steady rhythm is what
keeps the project sustainable and the operator un-burned-out. The durable policy behind
it is [PD-1](../decisions/pd-1-governance-and-contributions.md).

## How to report an issue

Post it in the tool's own thread in the Discord `#workbench` forum, rather than in DMs —
so the next person who hits the same thing finds your answer instead of the operator's
inbox. To track it down quickly without exhausting back-and-forth, please include:

1. **Which tool**, and roughly when it happened.
2. **What you expected**, and what you got instead.
3. **The relevant log** — for anything touching the game, that's
   `BepInEx/LogOutput.log` in your Valheim install. For a standalone tool, whatever
   it printed. Scrub anything that looks like a credential before pasting.

## What NOT to expect

To protect the people involved and honor the art this community makes, we protect
everyone's time — which means being honest about boundaries. During alpha, please
**don't expect**:

- **24/7 or instant fixes.** There are no SLAs or guaranteed uptime numbers. If a
  server goes down late at night, it may stay down until the next day.
- **Perfection.** The tools will break. That's not a failure of the community or
  the design; it's the physics of building something new.
- **Surveillance or mandates.** Nothing here audits you or forces a new way to
  play. These tools are opt-in — you use them only if they remove your toil. You
  own your outputs.

We're building this to give you relief, permission, and time. Thank you for
testing it with us.
