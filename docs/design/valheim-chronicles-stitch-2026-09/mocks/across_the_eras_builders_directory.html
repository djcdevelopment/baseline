Archival Preservation Codex

01 // Piece Attribution

Credits strictly follow creators recorded on saved construction pieces inside the dedicated Valheim world `.db` files. Shared albums may appear in multiple builders' threads if collaborative pieces exist.

02 // Independent Identities

Matching character names alone never automatically join accounts or character records across eras. Historical photo albums strictly retain their original attribution and realm links.

03 // Curatorial Access Logs

Search terms are archived in the web server's access log so future camera capture sessions can dynamically prioritize landmarks and longhouses that the community actively queries.
