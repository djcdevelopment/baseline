Tugcow's Blurb: About Me

Not every Viking needs to pierce the clouds with colossal spires. I build the honest hearths, the sturdy timber boathouses, the reinforced roof trusses that keep snow off the sleeping furs, and cozy village outposts where weary travelers dry their boots. Working with fine wood, core wood rafters, and humble stone footings since Era 7.
