B BASELINE

A toolkit to build an entire community on, in Valheim

With the things most hobbyist servers never get to have: identity, telemetry and testing as first-class parts of the stack instead of afterthoughts.

Server→Gateway→Player→Mod→BepInEx→Harmony→Unity→transpiling

The communities this is for already do this work — with mods, spreadsheets, Discord bots, checklists, screenshots. Baseline doesn't replace any of that. It's meant to let people spend less time on paperwork and tracking, and more time making things.

What the tools turn up

Photographing a world nobody had time to look at

Three million building pieces and one person. Clustering a save file into 1,833 structures, then placing a camera at every one of them from its geometry — 1,411 photographs, none of them framed by hand.

the arithmetic, the file contracts, and what each stage emits

From a leader's spreadsheet to a killing blow, and back with proof

A guild master keeps their tracker exactly as it is. The absorption engine harvests it verbatim, a player picks quests on a web page, and the game itself notices the completing blow and starts the paperwork — with a provenance page showing the leader every cell's journey.

the loop drawn end to end, the auto-capture machinery, and the seam that is not baked yet

Being straight with you

What is not open yet

The game server is not open. You can run every tool above on your own machine, but this is not yet an invitation to come play on the live world — the volunteer platform isn't ready, and saying otherwise would waste your evening. That work is tracked in the open on the roadmap. This is a solo-operator project in alpha, and it is written down honestly rather than dressed up.
