## Community source offer

An operated Community Deployment must link users to the complete source for
the exact deployed version. An unmodified deployment may link to the matching
tag or immutable commit in this repository. A modified deployment must link
to a public fork or archive containing the modifications and the build,
installation, configuration, and deployment material required by `LICENSE`.

Do not publish secrets, credentials, personal data, access tokens, private
keys, private diagnostics, or material you have no right to redistribute.
